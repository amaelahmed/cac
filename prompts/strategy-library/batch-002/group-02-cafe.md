# Batch 002 Group 02: Cafe Blocks

Generate draft blocks for local cafes and small food spots in India.

Business type: cafe / juice shop / bakery cafe / snack spot

Business context:

- Location: Indian local markets, college areas, office areas, tourist streets
- Customers: students, friend groups, families, office workers, tourists
- Platforms: Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business
- Goals: footfall, repeat visits, reviews, menu clarity, first-time discovery, delivery/pickup
- Budget: low to medium
- Common fears: taste mismatch, price, hygiene, small portion, bad ambience, hard to find location

Required sections:

- menu clarity cards
- local footfall cards
- Instagram food reels/posts
- WhatsApp/order replies
- customer problem cards
- Google Business/review cards
- repeat visit cards
- offer cards

Do not mix advice from salons, clothing stores, software, or gift shops.
