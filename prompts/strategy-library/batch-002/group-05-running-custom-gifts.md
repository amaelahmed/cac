# Batch 002 Group 05: Running Custom Product/Gift Blocks

Generate draft blocks for running customised product and gift businesses in India.

Business type: running custom gift/product store

Business context:

- Location: Indian local markets, including Kerala cities
- Customers: students, couples, friend groups, family gift buyers, corporate small orders
- Platforms: Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business
- Goals: more orders, repeat gift buyers, reviews, trust, delivery clarity, festival/occasion sales
- Budget: low to medium
- Common fears: wrong spelling, bad print quality, late delivery, unclear price, final product mismatch

Required sections:

- preview/approval cards
- material/finish cards
- Instagram reels/posts
- WhatsApp replies
- customer problem cards
- Google Business/review cards
- repeat occasion cards
- offer cards

Do not mention Cartroid unless the block uses `{{businessName}}`.
