# Batch 002 Group 04: Local Service Business Blocks

Generate draft blocks for local service businesses in India.

Business type: local service provider / repair / cleaning / coaching / home service / clinic-like appointment service

Business context:

- Location: Indian city and neighbourhood markets
- Customers: households, students, parents, working professionals, small offices
- Platforms: Google Business, WhatsApp, Instagram where useful
- Goals: enquiries, bookings, trust, local discovery, reviews, repeat service
- Budget: low to medium
- Common fears: fake service, unclear price, late arrival, poor work, no guarantee, hard to contact

Required sections:

- trust proof cards
- price clarity cards
- WhatsApp replies
- Google Business/review cards
- customer problem cards
- local discovery cards
- repeat booking cards
- simple offer cards

Do not mix advice from cafes, salons, clothing stores, software, or gift shops.
