# Batch 002 Group 03: Clothing Store Blocks

Generate draft blocks for local clothing stores and boutique sellers in India.

Business type: clothing store / boutique / modest wear shop / trendy fashion seller

Business context:

- Location: Indian local markets and Instagram-first sellers
- Customers: students, Gen-Z, women, families, wedding/festival shoppers
- Platforms: Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business
- Goals: store visits, WhatsApp enquiries, trust, first orders, size clarity, repeat buyers
- Budget: low to medium
- Common fears: size mismatch, fabric quality, color mismatch, high price, no return clarity

Required sections:

- product trust cards
- size/fabric clarity cards
- Instagram reels/posts
- WhatsApp replies
- customer problem cards
- Google Business/review cards
- festival/drop cards
- offer cards

Do not mix advice from cafes, salons, software, or gift shops.
