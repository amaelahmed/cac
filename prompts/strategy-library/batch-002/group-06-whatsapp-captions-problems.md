# Batch 002 Group 06: WhatsApp, Captions, Customer Problems

Generate cross-industry draft blocks for small Indian businesses.

Business type: reusable WhatsApp/caption/customer problem library for local businesses

Business context:

- Industries: salon, cafe, clothing, local service, custom products
- Platforms: WhatsApp, Instagram
- Goals: faster replies, clearer enquiries, more trust, fewer confused customers
- Budget: low
- Common fears: price, quality, timing, location, how to order, whether business is real

Required sections:

- WhatsApp first reply
- price reply
- delivery/appointment reply
- trust reply
- Instagram caption card
- customer problem card
- copy-ready text card

Each block must include tags that clearly restrict the industry or make the card safely reusable.
