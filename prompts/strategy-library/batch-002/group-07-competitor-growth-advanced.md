# Batch 002 Group 07: Competitor, Growth, Advanced Cards

Generate draft blocks for competitor cards, growth opportunities, and advanced growth plans.

Business type: local Indian small businesses

Business context:

- Industries: salon, cafe, clothing, local service, custom products
- Platforms: Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business
- Goals: stand out, get first customers, improve repeat sales, beat local alternatives
- Budget: low to medium
- Common competitor types: popular Instagram page, cheap local seller, established shop, bigger chain, marketplace seller

Required sections:

- competitor card
- competitive advantage card
- growth opportunity card
- advanced growth card
- simple measurement card
- local partnership card
- review/reputation card

Do not invent real competitor names. Use safe archetypes unless the business provides exact names.
