# Prompt Pack: Local Cafe

Create excellent original action-card blocks for a local cafe, coffee shop, bakery cafe, or small dine-in food business.

Business pattern:

- Stage: running or just opened
- Audience: students, local office crowd, families, friends
- Platforms: Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business
- Product: coffee, drinks, snacks, desserts, breakfast, ambience
- Main problems: weekday footfall, awareness, reviews, menu clarity, repeat visits, delivery/pickup, local competition

Create action cards that feel useful for a cafe owner.

Do not mention software, robots, book a call, LinkedIn, custom gifts, preview before making, or Cartroid.

Cover:

- menu hero item posts
- ambience proof
- Google review actions
- weekday offer cards
- local area posts
- WhatsApp pre-order messages
- repeat customer ideas
- customer worries
- competitor cards
- 30-day cafe calendar
