# Prompt Pack: Clothing Store

Create excellent original action-card blocks for a normal clothing store, boutique, or fashion retail shop.

Business pattern:

- Stage: running or newly opened
- Audience: local shoppers, students, families, young professionals
- Platforms: Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business
- Product: ready-made clothing, outfits, accessories, new arrivals
- Main problems: style trust, size doubt, price comparison, new arrival awareness, store visits, WhatsApp enquiries, reviews

Do not write custom gift advice unless the prompt explicitly says custom printing.
Do not mention Cartroid, preview before making, robot work, software, LinkedIn, or book a call.

Cover:

- new arrival cards
- outfit pairing posts
- size and fit clarity
- price range posts
- store visit reasons
- WhatsApp enquiry replies
- Google Business product photos
- customer worries
- competitor cards
- 30-day clothing calendar
