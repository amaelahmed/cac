# Prompt Pack: Local Service Business

Create excellent original action-card blocks for local service businesses.

Examples:

- repair service
- home service
- cleaning
- interior work
- photography
- event service
- coaching/service provider

Business pattern:

- Stage: running or just opened
- Audience: local individuals or small business owners
- Platforms: Google Business, WhatsApp, Instagram, website
- Main problems: trust, price/timing clarity, service area, booking friction, proof of work, reviews, follow-up

Do not mention custom gifts, Cartroid, software, robot work, LinkedIn, or office-hour savings unless the business is actually software.

Cover:

- enquiry/booking cards
- proof-of-work cards
- Google review actions
- service area clarity
- price/timing clarity
- before/after or problem/fix posts
- WhatsApp follow-ups
- customer worries
- competitor cards
- 30-day local service calendar
