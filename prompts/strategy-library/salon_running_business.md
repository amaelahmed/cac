# Prompt Pack: Running Salon

Create excellent original action-card blocks for a running salon, beauty studio, barber shop, skincare studio, or aesthetic service.

Business pattern:

- Stage: running business
- Audience: local individual customers
- Platforms: Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business
- Product: haircut, styling, beauty service, grooming, skincare, bridal/event services
- Main problems: trust in result, hygiene doubt, price confusion, appointment availability, before/after proof, repeat visits, reviews

Create action-card blocks that feel like a salon owner can use today.

Do not mention software, apps, robots, office hours, LinkedIn, custom gifts, Cartroid, or product shipping.

Cover:

- before/after result posts
- hygiene and setup proof
- booking messages
- service menu clarity
- Google review actions
- local search posts
- first-time visitor comfort
- repeat visit reminders
- stylist trust
- measurement cards
- 30-day content calendar
