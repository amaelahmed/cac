# Prompt Pack: Measurement Cards

Create simple measurement cards for small business owners.

Cover:

- pre-launch businesses
- running local businesses
- Instagram
- WhatsApp
- Google Business
- website

Each card should explain:

- what number to watch
- where to find it
- what good looks like
- what to do if it is weak
- how often to check it

Avoid analytics jargon. Keep it practical.
