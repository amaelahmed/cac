# Prompt Pack: 30-Day Content Calendars

Create excellent 30-day calendar blocks as action cards.

Each calendar card must include:

- day_hint
- post_type
- topic
- what_to_show
- ready_caption
- customer_action
- why_this_helps
- how_to_check

Create calendars for:

- pre-launch custom gifts
- running custom gifts
- salon
- cafe
- clothing store
- local service

Each calendar must feel specific to that business type. Do not mix business types.
