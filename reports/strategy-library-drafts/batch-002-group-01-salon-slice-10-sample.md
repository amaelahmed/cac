## Simple weekday student offer with clear ID check

- Section type: `student_offer_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: Students want a fair price but feel shy to ask. A simple weekday offer with a clear ID check makes them feel respected, not judged. No fake timer, no pressure.
- Do this: Pick one weekday like Tuesday or Wednesday for the student offer. Choose one service like basic haircut, simple cleanup, or threading. Set a clear starting price like {{startingPrice}} for students with valid college ID. Save the offer as a WhatsApp quick reply and a small poster at the billing counter. Train staff to ask for ID politely before confirming the price.
- Copy-ready text: Hi! Students with a valid college ID get {{serviceName}} at {{startingPrice}} on weekdays at {{businessName}}, {{location}}. Show your ID at the counter. No rush, book your slot here.
- Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} puts up a small card near the billing counter that says 'Student Offer - show ID, get basic haircut at {{startingPrice}} on Tuesday and Wednesday'. Staff ask for the ID before starting the service. The same line is saved as a WhatsApp quick reply for walk-in students who message.
- Why this works: Students trust clear prices more than hidden discounts. A weekday offer fills empty chairs without hurting weekend slots. Asking for ID keeps the offer honest and fair for real students.
- How to know it worked: Count how many students book or walk in on the chosen weekday. Ask each student how they heard about the offer when they visit.

## Mother-daughter family visit plan with set services and timing

- Section type: `family_package_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: Mothers and daughters often want to visit together but fear long waits. A simple family plan with two services, one chair pair, and a set time makes the visit calm and easy.
- Do this: Pick two services that work well together, like haircut for mom and simple facial for daughter. Set a fixed family slot on a slow day like a weekday afternoon. Block two chairs side by side or one chair pair for the family. Write the package as 'Mom + Daughter' or 'Family of 2' with a starting price like {{startingPrice}}. Save the package as a WhatsApp quick reply and a small printed card at the counter.
- Copy-ready text: Mom and Daughter package at {{businessName}}, {{location}}. Two chairs, one time slot, services: {{serviceName}}. Starting at {{startingPrice}}. Book a weekday slot on WhatsApp.
- Example: {{businessName}} keeps a small printed card at the counter: 'Mother-Daughter Plan - haircut for mom, simple cleanup for daughter, two chairs side by side, starting at {{startingPrice}}'. When a customer asks for a family visit on WhatsApp, the staff sends the same line as a quick reply and blocks two chairs for the chosen weekday afternoon.
- Why this works: Mothers feel safe when they know the timing. Daughters feel special when the visit is planned for them. A fixed slot stops the salon from overbooking and keeps the visit smooth.
- How to know it worked: Count how many family bookings come in each week. Ask at checkout how the family heard about the package.

## Men grooming service trust card with hygiene and WhatsApp booking

- Section type: `men_grooming_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: grooming_studio
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: Men who visit a salon for the first time fear a bad cut, dirty tools, or being judged. A short trust card that shows hygiene steps and a simple WhatsApp booking line removes the fear before they walk in.
- Do this: List 3 main men services like haircut, beard trim, and clean shave with starting price {{startingPrice}} each. Take 3 short photos: clean tools on a tray, fresh towel for each customer, and a barber wiping the chair. Post the photos on Instagram and save them in WhatsApp catalogue. Write one WhatsApp reply that lists the services and asks for the preferred time. Keep a small printed card at the counter with the same three services and prices.
- Copy-ready text: Men grooming at {{businessName}}, {{location}}. Haircut, beard trim, clean shave from {{startingPrice}}. Fresh towel, clean tools, one customer at a time. Send your preferred time here to book.
- Example: {{businessName}} keeps three photos ready in the WhatsApp catalogue: one of tools dipped in sanitiser, one of a fresh white towel, and one of the barber wiping the chair. When a man messages asking about grooming, the staff sends the three photos plus the service list in one short message and asks for a preferred time slot. The same three photos go up as an Instagram post with the caption 'How we set up before every customer'.
- Why this works: Men want clear answers fast. Showing the cleaning steps before they ask builds trust. A simple WhatsApp booking line saves them a phone call and stops no-shows.
- How to know it worked: Count how many men book through WhatsApp in a week. Ask new men customers at checkout what made them pick this salon.
