## Simple first-time visitor offer without fake urgency

- Section type: `simple_offer_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A small, honest offer for first-time visitors helps new customers try your salon without feeling trapped. No countdown timers, no fake hurry.
- Do this: Pick one service for the first-time offer, like a basic haircut or simple facial. Keep the offer open all week, not just for 2 hours. Write the offer in plain words: what they get, what they save. Share the offer on your Instagram page and WhatsApp catalogue. Tell the staff to mention the offer when a new customer walks in or messages.
- Copy-ready text: First time at {{businessName}}? Get {{serviceName}} at a special price. No rush, valid all week. Show this message at the counter. - {{location}}
- Example: A salon in Kozhikode adds this offer to their Instagram bio and WhatsApp catalogue. When a new customer messages asking about prices, the staff sends this line along with the menu.
- Why this works: New customers are scared to try a new salon. A small, clear offer lowers that fear and gives them a reason to walk in this week.
- How to know it worked: New customers mention the offer when they walk in or when they message on WhatsApp before booking.
