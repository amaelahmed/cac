## Show your stylist's consultation step before the service starts

- Section type: `stylist_trust_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A short consultation before the service helps the customer feel heard. It also shows your stylist listens and plans, not just cuts fast.
- Do this: Before any haircut or colour, ask the customer 3 questions: what length, what look, any past problems. Write the answers in a small notepad near the chair. Show the customer 2-3 photos of similar work you have done before. Ask one more time: 'Shall I start with this plan?' Keep the notepad on the counter so staff follow the same steps every time.
- Copy-ready text: Hi, before we start, can I ask 3 quick questions? 1) What length do you want? 2) Any hair problem like dandruff or hair fall? 3) Show me a photo of the look you like. I will plan the steps for you. - {{businessName}}
- Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the stylist keeps a small notepad by every chair. Before a haircut, she writes the customer's answer to length, look, and past hair issues. She shows two past photos of similar hair from her phone. Then she asks if the plan is okay. The customer sees the plan before scissors touch her hair.
- Why this works: Most bad haircut stories start when the stylist jumps in without asking. A 2-minute talk removes the fear of being misunderstood. It also makes the stylist look trained and careful, not rushed.
- How to know it worked: Customers start saying things like 'you understood what I wanted' or 'no one asked me this before'. You will see fewer re-dos and fewer complaints about the result.

## Polite WhatsApp message when you are running late

- Section type: `delay_message_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: When your chair or stylist is running late, a quick honest WhatsApp message keeps trust. Hiding the delay makes customers angry. A simple update makes them wait calmly.
- Do this: Send the message as soon as you know the delay, not 20 minutes later. Keep the message short: sorry, reason, new time, ask if it still works. Offer two choices: wait a bit or shift to a later slot. Save this message as a WhatsApp Business quick reply so you can send in 5 seconds. Update the customer again if the delay grows by more than 15 minutes.
- Copy-ready text: Hi, sorry, your slot is running about {{delay_minutes}} mins late at {{businessName}}. You can wait with chai, or I can shift you to {{new_time}}. Which works for you?
- Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, when a colour service takes longer than planned, the front desk sends the saved WhatsApp message right away. The customer picks: wait or shift. If she waits and the delay grows, the desk sends one more short update. No customer is left guessing in the chair.
- Why this works: Customers fear waiting without knowing why. A clear, honest message with two choices makes them feel respected. Most pick waiting once they know the time.
- How to know it worked: Fewer customers leave without service. Front desk gets less 'where are you?' calls. Customers reply with 'okay, I will wait' instead of cancelling.

## Simple aftercare card for hair and facial services

- Section type: `aftercare_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: Customers forget what to do at home after a facial or hair colour. A short written card or WhatsApp note stops damage and cuts repeat calls asking what to do.
- Do this: Pick the 3 most common services you do: hair colour, facial, haircut with styling. Write 3 simple aftercare points for each on a small card or phone note. Give the card after the service, before the customer pays. Also send the same points on WhatsApp the same evening. Add one line: 'Message us if you see any skin reaction or hair issue in 2 days.'
- Copy-ready text: After your {{serviceName}} today: 1) Do not wash hair for {{hours}} hours. 2) Do not use hot water on face for 1 day. 3) Avoid sun and sweat for 2 days. Message {{businessName}} on WhatsApp if you see any reaction. - Team {{businessName}}
- Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, after a hair colour service, the stylist hands a small printed card with 3 lines. She also sends the same 3 lines on WhatsApp at 7 pm. The card has the salon WhatsApp number at the bottom. If the customer gets an itch or rash, she knows who to message.
- Why this works: Most aftercare problems happen because the customer forgot the verbal advice. A written card plus WhatsApp note repeats the same message twice. It also lowers the fear of skin reaction because the customer knows who to contact.
- How to know it worked: You get fewer 'what do I do?' calls after service. Customers come back sooner for the next visit. You see fewer skin reaction complaints because people contact you early.
