## WhatsApp booking message that confirms time and seat

- Section type: `booking_trust_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: When a customer asks for a slot on WhatsApp, reply fast with the time, the service, and the seat. This stops double-bookings and calms the fear of waiting.
- Do this: Open WhatsApp Business and keep the chat reply short. Type the service name, the time slot, and ask 'Shall I fix this for you?' Save this reply as a quick reply on WhatsApp Business so you can send it in 2 seconds. Send the salon address pin from your location card after they say yes.
- Copy-ready text: Hi {{customerName}}, your {{serviceName}} is fixed for {{timeSlot}} at {{businessName}}, {{location}}. Please come 5 minutes early. Reply YES to confirm. Thank you!
- Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the owner saves this message as WhatsApp Quick Reply #1. When a customer messages for a haircut slot, she taps the quick reply, adds the time, and sends. She then sends the Google Maps pin from the business profile.
- Why this works: Customers want a clear yes-or-no answer. A short message with time, service, and place removes confusion and stops no-shows. Quick replies save the owner typing the same thing 10 times a day.
- How to know it worked: Check your WhatsApp chat at end of day. Count how many bookings were confirmed with a YES reply. You can also check your appointment register for fewer walk-in queries asking 'Is my slot still on?'

## Show fresh tools and clean towels in a 20-second reel

- Section type: `hygiene_proof_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A short reel of clean tools, fresh towels, and a wiped chair makes customers trust your salon before they visit. It answers the biggest fear: 'Is it clean?'
- Do this: Pick one chair and one tool tray. Wipe them in front of the camera. Take a fresh towel from the stack and place it on the chair. Dip the comb and scissors in a sanitiser jar for 2 seconds, on camera. Film in vertical mode, 20 seconds, with a small caption on top. Post as a Reel with hashtags like #CleanSalon #{{location}}Salon.
- Copy-ready text: Fresh towel. Wiped chair. Sanitised tools. Every customer, every time at {{businessName}}. Walk in with confidence.
- Example: The stylist at {{businessName}} films a 20-second reel every Monday morning before opening. She posts it with the caption above and tags the local area. New followers see clean setup before they book.
- Why this works: Most salon fears are about hygiene, not skill. Showing the cleaning step on video removes the guesswork. Customers who see clean tools are more likely to walk in for the first time.
- How to know it worked: Look at the Reel views after 48 hours and count new profile visits. Ask walk-in customers 'Where did you hear about us?' and note if they say Instagram.

## WhatsApp price list reply for 'How much?' messages

- Section type: `whatsapp_reply_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: When customers ask about price, send a short list with service names and starting prices. No long chat, no surprise at the counter.
- Do this: Make a simple price list with 6 to 8 main services and starting prices like {{startingPrice}}. Open WhatsApp Business, go to Settings > Business Tools > Catalogue, and add each service with a short note. Save a quick reply that says 'Here is our price list' and attach the catalogue link. Send this quick reply every time a customer asks 'How much?' or 'Rate?'.
- Copy-ready text: Hi! Here is our price list at {{businessName}}, {{location}}. Haircut starts at {{startingPrice}}. Facial starts at {{startingPrice}}. Full list is in our catalogue: {{catalogueLink}}. Reply with the service you want and we will share the next slot.
- Example: At {{businessName}}, the receptionist gets 15 'rate' messages every day. She saved the message above as Quick Reply #2. She types 'rate' in chat and taps the quick reply. The catalogue link opens inside WhatsApp with all prices.
- Why this works: Customers hate price surprises at the billing counter. A clear list sent in the first reply builds trust and saves 5 minutes of back-and-forth typing. The catalogue link keeps the message short.
- How to know it worked: Check WhatsApp Business stats for quick reply usage. Count how many 'rate' chats move to a booking slot in the same day. Ask new customers if the price matched what they saw on WhatsApp.
