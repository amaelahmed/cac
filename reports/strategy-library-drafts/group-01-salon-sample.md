## Show your cleaning routine in a short video

- Section type: `hygiene_proof_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A 30-second video of how you clean tools and chairs removes the main fear customers have before visiting.
- Do this: Film your staff wiping the chair, swapping towels, and dipping tools in sanitiser before a customer sits. Add a small caption on the video: 'How we clean before every customer at {{businessName}}. ' Post it on Instagram Reels and also send it as a WhatsApp Status. Save the video on your phone so you can send it on WhatsApp when someone asks 'Is your place clean?'
- Copy-ready text: Hi! Here is how we clean before every customer at {{businessName}} in {{location}}. Fresh towel, sanitised tools, wiped chair. You can watch and decide. 
- Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} films a quick clip every morning showing the cleaning steps. They keep the video saved on their phone and share it in WhatsApp chat whenever a new customer asks about hygiene before booking.
- Why this works: Most salon worries are about hygiene. When you show the cleaning step on camera, the customer feels safe before she even arrives.
- How to know it worked: New customers mention the cleaning video in WhatsApp chat or reply 'okay, I saw your video' before confirming a visit.

## Reply fast on WhatsApp with a clear price list

- Section type: `whatsapp_reply_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: When customers ask 'How much?', they want a clear answer in one message. A ready price list saves you time and stops the price surprise fear.
- Do this: Save a price list on your phone with service name and starting price like {{startingPrice}}. Open WhatsApp Business and put the list in 'Catalogue' so it is one tap away. When someone messages asking the price, reply with the catalogue card and one line. Add a line at the end asking which day they want to come.
- Copy-ready text: Hi! Here is our price list for {{serviceName}} starting at {{startingPrice}}. Which day works for you - weekday or weekend? 
- Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} gets many 'rate?' messages on WhatsApp. They tap the Catalogue card with haircut, facial, and threading prices and add one line asking for the day. This saves typing the same reply again and again.
- Why this works: Customers decide fast when they see the price in the first reply. A clear list also stops them worrying about extra charges later.
- How to know it worked: You stop typing long price messages and customers reply with a day and time instead of asking more questions.

## Ask for a Google review right after the service

- Section type: `review_request_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A short friendly ask at the right moment helps you get more Google reviews, which brings new local customers searching nearby.
- Do this: Pick a happy customer right after the service ends and before she pays. Say the review line below and show the Google Maps page on your phone. Keep a printed card with the Google review link near the billing counter. Reply to every new review with a short thank you within two days.
- Copy-ready text: If you liked the service today, please tap stars and write a few lines on Google. It really helps {{businessName}} in {{location}}. Thank you! 
- Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} keeps a small standee at the billing counter with the Google review QR code. After a haircut or facial, the staff gently asks the customer to scan and review before leaving.
- Why this works: New customers in your area search Google Maps before picking a salon. More real reviews push you above other shops nearby and build trust fast.
- How to know it worked: New Google reviews come in each week and your salon starts showing up higher in Maps when people search 'salon near me'.
