### Show your cleaning routine in a short video

A 30-second video of how you clean tools and chairs removes the main fear customers have before visiting.

- Film your staff wiping the chair, swapping towels, and dipping tools in sanitiser before a customer sits.
- Add a small caption on the video: 'How we clean before every customer at {{businessName}}.'
- Post it on Instagram Reels and also send it as a WhatsApp Status.
- Save the video on your phone so you can send it on WhatsApp when someone asks 'Is your place clean?'

Copy: Hi! Here is how we clean before every customer at {{businessName}} in {{location}}. Fresh towel, sanitised tools, wiped chair. You can watch and decide.

Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} films a quick clip before opening that shows the chair, towel, and tool cleaning steps. The owner saves the same video for WhatsApp replies about hygiene.

### Reply fast on WhatsApp with a clear price list

When customers ask 'How much?', they want a clear answer in one message. A ready price list saves you time and stops the price surprise fear.

- Save a price list on your phone with service name and starting price like {{startingPrice}}.
- Open WhatsApp Business and put the list in Catalogue so it is one tap away.
- When someone messages asking the price, reply with the catalogue card and one line.
- Add a line at the end asking which day they want to come.

Copy: Hi! Here is our price list for {{serviceName}} starting at {{startingPrice}}. Which day works for you, weekday or weekend?

Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} sets up a WhatsApp Catalogue with haircut, facial, and threading prices. The owner sends the catalogue card first, then asks which day the customer wants to visit.

### Ask for a Google review right after the service

A short friendly ask at the right moment helps you collect real Google reviews from customers who already had a good visit.

- Pick a happy customer right after the service ends and before she pays.
- Say the review line below and show the Google Maps page on your phone.
- Keep a printed card with the Google review link near the billing counter.
- Reply to every new review with a short thank you within two days.

Copy: If you liked the service today, please tap stars and write a few lines on Google. It really helps {{businessName}} in {{location}}. Thank you!

Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} keeps a small standee at the billing counter with the Google review QR code. After a haircut or facial, the staff gently asks the customer to scan and review before leaving.

### WhatsApp booking message that confirms time and seat

When a customer asks for a slot on WhatsApp, reply fast with the time, the service, and the seat. This stops double-bookings and calms the fear of waiting.

- Open WhatsApp Business and keep the chat reply short.
- Type the service name, the time slot, and ask 'Shall I fix this for you?'
- Save this reply as a quick reply on WhatsApp Business so you can send it in 2 seconds.
- Send the salon address pin from your location card after they say yes.

Copy: Hi {{customerName}}, your {{serviceName}} is fixed for {{timeSlot}} at {{businessName}}, {{location}}. Please come 5 minutes early. Reply YES to confirm. Thank you!

Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the owner saves this as a WhatsApp Quick Reply. For each booking, she fills in the service, time, and map pin before sending it.

### Show your patch test and fix-it plan for skin fear

Many customers worry their skin will react to a new product. A short, clear message about your patch test and safety step calms them before they book.

- Add one line on your WhatsApp catalogue: 'Tell us if you had any skin reaction before.'
- Keep your patch-test step written near the billing counter so staff repeat it the same way.
- If a customer asks about skin reaction, reply with the message below.
- Save the reply as a quick reply on WhatsApp Business.

Copy: Hi, before we use a new product, we first ask about past skin reactions and do a small patch test if needed. If it does not feel right, we stop and choose a milder option. - {{businessName}}

Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the staff saves this as a WhatsApp quick reply. When someone asks 'Will this suit my skin?', they send the reply and explain the patch-test step before booking.

### Post a real before-and-after with a clear honest caption

A real before-and-after photo with a simple honest caption builds trust. No filters, no number claims, just the work and the steps you used.

- Ask the customer if you can share her photo. Only post after she says yes.
- Take two photos in the same spot and same light: one before, one after.
- Write a caption that lists the service, the steps, and the time taken.
- Add your city and your WhatsApp booking line in the caption.
- Keep the photo natural, without beauty filters.

Copy: Before and after at {{businessName}}, {{location}}. Service: {{serviceName}}. Steps: cleanse, trim, shape, finish. Time: {{serviceTime}}. Booking open on WhatsApp. No filters used.

Example: At {{businessName}}, the stylist asks permission, takes the after photo in the same chair and light as the before photo, and posts it with the caption above. No customer name is shown.

A short WhatsApp message after the service tells the customer when to come back. It keeps you in her mind without being pushy.

- After the customer pays, note the next visit date in a small diary or phone note.
- Save a reminder message on WhatsApp Business as a quick reply.
- Send the message the same evening or the next morning.
- Keep it short: service name, date suggestion, and a booking link.
- Do not send more than one reminder per visit.

### Simple first-time visitor offer without fake urgency

A small, honest offer for first-time visitors helps new customers try your salon without feeling trapped. No countdown timers, no fake hurry.

- Pick one service for the first-time offer, like a basic haircut or simple facial.
- Keep the offer open all week, not just for 2 hours.
- Write the offer in plain words: what they get, what they save.
- Share the offer on your Instagram page and WhatsApp catalogue.
- Tell the staff to mention the offer when a new customer walks in or messages.

Copy: First time at {{businessName}}? Get {{serviceName}} at a special price. No rush, valid all week. Show this message at the counter. - {{location}}

### Show the consultation step before the service starts

A short consultation before the service helps the customer feel heard. It shows your stylist listens and plans before starting.

- Before any haircut or colour, ask the customer three questions: what length, what look, and any past hair problem.
- Write the answers in a small notepad near the chair.
- Show two similar work photos from your phone if you have permission to use them.
- Ask one final question: 'Shall I start with this plan?'
- Keep the same question list at every chair so staff follow the same process.

Copy: Before we start, can I ask 3 quick questions? What length do you want? Any hair issue? Do you have a photo of the look you like? I will plan the steps first. - {{businessName}}

Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the stylist keeps a small notepad by the chair and writes the customer's length, look, and past hair issue before starting the service.

### Send a polite WhatsApp message when the slot is late

When your chair or stylist is running late, a quick honest WhatsApp message keeps trust. Hiding the delay makes customers more upset.

- Send the message as soon as you know the delay.
- Keep it short: sorry, reason, new time, and a choice.
- Offer two options: wait for the next chair or shift to a later slot.
- Save this as a WhatsApp Business quick reply.
- If the delay becomes longer, send one more short update.

Copy: Hi, sorry. Your slot at {{businessName}} is running about {{delayMinutes}} minutes late. You can wait, or I can shift you to {{newTime}}. Which is better for you?

Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, when a colour service takes longer than planned, the front desk sends this saved WhatsApp reply and lets the customer choose whether to wait or shift.
