## Show your cleaning steps before the customer sits down

- Section type: `hygiene_proof_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A short video of you cleaning tools and chair before each customer removes the main fear people have before visiting a salon.
- Do this: Film a 30-second video on your phone. Start with the chair being wiped with a clean cloth. Show tools going into sanitiser liquid. Show a fresh towel being placed. Add a small caption on the video with your salon name.
- Copy-ready text: How we clean before every customer at {{businessName}}. Fresh towel, sanitised tools, wiped chair. Watch before you book.
- Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} films the cleaning steps once before opening. They post this reel on Instagram and pin it to the top of their page so new customers see it first.
- Why this works: Most new customers worry about hygiene before they book. Seeing real cleaning steps builds trust faster than any text post.
- How to know it worked: New customers mention the video in WhatsApp chat or in person before sitting down.

## Keep a clear price list ready on WhatsApp Catalogue

- Section type: `whatsapp_reply_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: When customers ask 'How much?' on WhatsApp, a ready price list in Catalogue gives them a clear answer in one tap.
- Do this: Open WhatsApp Business on your phone. Tap Catalogue at the bottom. Add each service with a short name and starting price like {{startingPrice}}. Add one photo for each service if you have one. Pin the catalogue link in your business bio.
- Copy-ready text: Hi, here is our price list 👇 {{serviceName}} starts at {{startingPrice}}. Tap our Catalogue to see all services.
- Example: {{businessName}} adds haircut, facial, and threading to WhatsApp Catalogue. When a new customer asks for prices in chat, they send the catalogue link instead of typing each price.
- Why this works: Customers want a fast clear answer. A ready catalogue saves you typing time and removes the price surprise fear.
- How to know it worked: You stop getting repeated price questions in chat and customers reply with the service they want.

## Ask for a Google review right after the service ends

- Section type: `review_request_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: Asking for a Google review at the right moment helps you collect real reviews from happy customers.
- Do this: Pick a happy customer just after the service ends. Smile and say the review line below. Open Google Maps on your phone. Search your salon name and tap the review button. Hand the phone to the customer for 30 seconds.
- Copy-ready text: Glad you liked the {{serviceName}} 🙏 If you have 30 seconds, please leave a Google review for {{businessName}}. It really helps us.
- Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} keeps Google Maps ready on the counter. After a haircut, the stylist asks the customer once and shows the review page on the shop tablet.
- Why this works: Customers are happiest right after a good service. A simple ask at this moment feels natural and not pushy.
- How to know it worked: New Google reviews show up on your business listing within a few days.
