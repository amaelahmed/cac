## Show your patch test and fix-it plan for skin fear

- Section type: `customer_problem_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: Many customers worry their skin will react to a new product. A short, clear message about your patch test and fix policy calms them before they book.
- Do this: Add one line on your WhatsApp catalogue: 'We do a patch test for sensitive skin. Tell us if you had any reaction before.' Keep a small bottle of soothing lotion at the billing counter so customers can see it. If a customer asks about skin reaction, reply with the message below. Save the reply as a quick reply on WhatsApp Business.
- Copy-ready text: Hi, before we start any new product on you, we do a small patch test behind the ear. If your skin reacts, we stop and use a milder option. You can come in tomorrow for a free check. - {{businessName}}
- Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the staff open WhatsApp, pick the saved quick reply, and send it the moment a customer asks 'Will this suit my skin?'. The customer sees the patch test step before she books, so the fear is answered before she sits in the chair.
- Why this works: Telling the customer what you will do removes the unknown. A patch test is a small step that shows you care about her skin, not just the service.
- How to know it worked: Customers stop asking long questions about skin reaction in chat. More first-time customers book without asking for a discount.

## Post a real before-and-after with a clear honest caption

- Section type: `instagram_content_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A real before-and-after photo with a simple honest caption builds trust. No filters, no number claims, just the work and the steps you used.
- Do this: Ask the customer if you can share her photo. Only post after she says yes. Take two photos in the same spot, same light: one before, one after. Write a caption that lists the service, the steps, and the time taken. Add your city and your booking link in the caption. Post between 6 pm and 8 pm when local followers are free.
- Copy-ready text: Before and after at {{businessName}}, {{location}}. Service: {{serviceName}}. Steps: cleanse, trim, shape, finish. Time: 45 min. Booking open on WhatsApp. No filters used. #{{location}}Salon
- Example: At {{businessName}}, the stylist finishes a haircut, asks the customer 'Can I share this photo? No name will be shown.', takes the after photo in the same chair and light as the before, and posts it the same evening with the caption above. The next day, two new followers send a DM asking for the price list.
- Why this works: Real photos with honest captions beat filtered ads. Local customers can see the work, the time, and the place, so they trust it more.
- How to know it worked: You get DMs asking 'How much?' and 'Can I book?' on the post. New followers from your city go up over the week.

## Send a simple next-visit reminder on WhatsApp

- Section type: `repeat_visit_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A short WhatsApp message after the service tells the customer when to come back. It keeps you in her mind without being pushy.
- Do this: After the customer pays, note the next visit date in a small diary or phone note. Save a reminder message on WhatsApp Business as a quick reply. Send the message the same evening or the next morning. Keep it short: service name, date suggestion, and a booking link. Do not send more than one reminder per visit.
- Copy-ready text: Hi, this is {{businessName}}. Your {{serviceName}} is good for the next 3 to 4 weeks. Want me to fix a slot on {{date}}? Reply yes and I will book it. - {{businessName}}
- Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the staff writes the next visit date on a small card and also saves it in the phone. After the customer leaves, they open WhatsApp, pick the saved quick reply, and send it that night. The customer replies 'yes' or picks another day, and the slot is fixed without a phone call.
- Why this works: Customers forget when to come back. A short note from the salon feels helpful, not salesy, and fills empty slots before the week ends.
- How to know it worked: More customers book the next visit within the same week. Your weekly diary shows fewer empty slots on slow days.
