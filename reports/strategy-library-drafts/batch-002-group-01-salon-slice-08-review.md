### Show your stylist's consultation step before the service starts

A short consultation before the service helps the customer feel heard. It also shows your stylist listens and plans, not just cuts fast.

- Before any haircut or colour, ask the customer 3 questions: what length, what look, any past problems.
- Write the answers in a small notepad near the chair.
- Show the customer 2-3 photos of similar work you have done before.
- Ask one more time: 'Shall I start with this plan?'
- Keep the notepad on the counter so staff follow the same steps every time.

Copy: Hi, before we start, can I ask 3 quick questions? 1) What length do you want? 2) Any hair problem like dandruff or hair fall? 3) Show me a photo of the look you like. I will plan the steps for you. - {{businessName}}

Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the stylist keeps a small notepad by every chair. Before a haircut, she writes the customer's answer to length, look, and past hair issues. She shows two past photos of similar hair from her phone. Then she asks if the plan is okay. The customer sees the plan before scissors touch her hair.

### Polite WhatsApp message when you are running late

When your chair or stylist is running late, a quick honest WhatsApp message keeps trust. Hiding the delay makes customers angry. A simple update makes them wait calmly.

- Send the message as soon as you know the delay, not 20 minutes later.
- Keep the message short: sorry, reason, new time, ask if it still works.
- Offer two choices: wait a bit or shift to a later slot.
- Save this message as a WhatsApp Business quick reply so you can send in 5 seconds.
- Update the customer again if the delay grows by more than 15 minutes.

Copy: Hi, sorry, your slot is running about {{delay_minutes}} mins late at {{businessName}}. You can wait with chai, or I can shift you to {{new_time}}. Which works for you?

Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, when a colour service takes longer than planned, the front desk sends the saved WhatsApp message right away. The customer picks: wait or shift. If she waits and the delay grows, the desk sends one more short update. No customer is left guessing in the chair.

### Simple aftercare card for hair and facial services

Customers forget what to do at home after a facial or hair colour. A short written card or WhatsApp note stops damage and cuts repeat calls asking what to do.

- Pick the 3 most common services you do: hair colour, facial, haircut with styling.
- Write 3 simple aftercare points for each on a small card or phone note.
- Give the card after the service, before the customer pays.
- Also send the same points on WhatsApp the same evening.
- Add one line: 'Message us if you see any skin reaction or hair issue in 2 days.'

Copy: After your {{serviceName}} today: 1) Do not wash hair for {{hours}} hours. 2) Do not use hot water on face for 1 day. 3) Avoid sun and sweat for 2 days. Message {{businessName}} on WhatsApp if you see any reaction. - Team {{businessName}}

Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, after a hair colour service, the stylist hands a small printed card with 3 lines. She also sends the same 3 lines on WhatsApp at 7 pm. The card has the salon WhatsApp number at the bottom. If the customer gets an itch or rash, she knows who to message.
