## Ask a happy customer to refer a friend without promising rewards you cannot afford

- Section type: `referral_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: A polite ask at the right moment gets real referrals. You do not need a big reward. A simple thank-you and a small honest note is enough.
- Do this: Pick a customer who smiled and said she liked the service, just before she pays. Say the referral line below in a calm friendly tone. Hand her one business card or save her number for a WhatsApp follow-up after 3 days. Write down the name of the friend she mentions in a small referral diary so you can track who came from where. Do not promise free service or cash. If you want to give something, keep it small like 10 percent off her next visit.
- Copy-ready text: Thank you so much. If you have a friend or sister who would like a visit, please send her our way. We will take good care of her too.
- Example: At {{businessName}} in {{location}}, the stylist finishes a haircut, the customer smiles and says she loves it. Before the customer walks to the billing counter, the stylist says the referral line and hands over one card. The owner writes the customer name and the friend name in a small notebook. After 3 days, a short WhatsApp is sent asking if the friend visited.
- Why this works: Happy customers want to help but forget if you do not ask. A short polite ask at the right moment is easy for her to act on. A small notebook helps you see which referrals actually came, without spending money on ads.
- How to know it worked: New customers mention the name of the friend who sent them. You can match this to the names in your referral notebook.

## Plan Vishu, Eid, Onam and wedding season bookings with honest limited slots

- Section type: `festival_booking_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: running
- Simple explanation: Festival days and wedding season bring many booking requests. Honest slot planning keeps your staff fresh and your service quality high. No fake countdown timers.
- Do this: Make a list of festival and wedding days in your area for the next 3 months. Write them on one page near the billing counter. Decide how many bridal or long-service slots you can take per festival day based on your staff count. For example, 3 to 4 bridal slots per day, not more. Open bookings 2 to 3 weeks before the festival. Tell customers slots are limited because of the festival rush, but do not use countdown timers or fake hurry. Ask each booking customer to pay a small advance like {{startingPrice}} to hold the slot. Write the advance in your diary against her name. Send a WhatsApp reminder 2 days before and 1 day before the booked slot.
- Copy-ready text: Hi, thank you for booking with {{businessName}}. For {{festivalName}} we have limited slots this year. Please share your preferred date and time. A small advance will hold your slot.
- Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} lists Onam, Eid and the wedding months on a paper near the billing counter. The owner decides 4 bridal slots per festival day. Bookings open 3 weeks before Onam. Each bride pays a small advance and gets a WhatsApp reminder 2 days before. No countdown timer is posted anywhere.
- Why this works: Customers respect clear limits more than fake urgency. Honest slots protect your staff from overwork and keep service quality steady during busy days. Advance payment reduces last-minute cancellations.
- How to know it worked: Your diary shows the number of festival slots filled before the festival week. You can also check how many customers followed the advance rule.

## Help nearby customers find your salon using Google, landmarks and WhatsApp location

- Section type: `local_discovery_card`
- Quality score: 5
- Business type: salon
- Launch status: early_stage
- Simple explanation: Most salon customers search on Google or ask for directions on WhatsApp. A clear Google profile, a simple landmark line and a WhatsApp location pin removes confusion for new visitors.
- Do this: Open your Google Business profile on your phone. Add your full address, pin the exact location on the map, and add 8 to 10 real photos of the salon front, seating area and finished work. In the business name field, add your area or landmark, like {{businessName}} near {{landmark}} in {{location}}. This helps people searching the area find you. Write 3 short direction lines using landmarks. Save them as a quick reply on WhatsApp Business. Example: 'We are 2 minutes from {{landmark}}. Look for the blue board, first floor.' When a customer asks for directions on WhatsApp, send your live location pin using the WhatsApp location button. Ask 5 regular customers to check your Google listing and confirm the address is correct.
- Copy-ready text: Hi, we are at {{businessName}}, {{fullAddress}}. We are near {{landmark}}. Tap the location pin I am sending to reach us easily.
- Example: {{businessName}} in {{location}} updates the Google profile with photos, full address and area name. The owner saves 3 landmark-based direction lines as WhatsApp quick replies. When a new customer asks 'how to reach?', the owner sends the quick reply and then sends the live WhatsApp location pin. Five regular customers are asked to open Google Maps and check if the pin is in the right spot.
- Why this works: Most local customers search 'salon near me' on Google or ask friends for landmarks. A clear Google profile plus a simple landmark line makes your salon easy to find without paid ads. WhatsApp location pin saves the customer time and reduces wrong turns.
- How to know it worked: New customers say they found you on Google Maps or reached the salon using the WhatsApp pin without asking for more directions.
