### Book a bridal makeup trial the simple way

A trial visit lets the bride see the full look before the wedding day. She can change the style, check for skin reaction, and feel calm about the final day. No payment claim, no promise of perfect look, just a clear plan.

- Set one fixed trial slot per week so you are not rushed.
- Ask the bride to bring 1 or 2 reference photos on her phone.
- Tell her the trial takes about 1.5 to 2 hours for makeup and hair.
- Do a small patch test on her jawline 24 hours before the trial if she has sensitive skin.
- Write down the products used and the look you agreed on in a small notebook.
- Share the agreed look summary on WhatsApp after the trial so she can show family.

Copy: Hi, this is {{businessName}} in {{location}}. For your bridal trial, please share 1-2 reference photos. Trial takes about 2 hours. Patch test is included. Trial slot: {{trialDate}} at {{trialTime}}. Shall I fix it?

Example: A bride messages on WhatsApp asking about bridal makeup. You reply with the trial message, fix a Saturday morning slot, and ask her to send reference photos the night before. During the trial you note the products used and the agreed style. After the trial you send a short WhatsApp summary with the look details so she can discuss with family.

### Turn a walk-in into a fixed booking for a longer service

Walk-in customers often want a long service like colour, smoothening, or bridal prep. Booking the slot protects their time and your chair. A short chat at the counter is enough to fix the visit.

- Greet the walk-in customer and ask which service she wants.
- Check the chair and stylist time needed for that service.
- Offer the next free slot today or tomorrow, not a vague time.
- Write her name, phone number, service, and slot in the appointment diary.
- Send a short WhatsApp confirmation with date, time, and service name.
- Ask her to reply with a yes so the slot is locked.

Copy: Hi {{customerName}}, your slot at {{businessName}} is fixed for {{bookingDate}} at {{bookingTime}} for {{serviceName}}. Please reply YES to confirm. Thank you.

Example: A woman walks in at 11am and asks for hair smoothening. You tell her the service takes about 3 hours and the next free slot is tomorrow at 2pm. She agrees, you write her name and number in the diary, and you send the WhatsApp confirmation before she leaves the shop. She replies YES the same evening.

### Polite message when a customer misses her appointment

When a customer does not show up, a polite message keeps the door open. No scolding, no public call-out. One short WhatsApp message is enough to reschedule and protect the slot for the next customer.

- Wait 15 minutes past the slot time before messaging.
- Send one short WhatsApp message asking if she is still coming.
- Wait 30 more minutes for a reply.
- If no reply, send a second message offering to reschedule.
- Mark the slot as open in your diary after the second message.
- Keep the tone soft and helpful, not angry or blaming.

Copy: Hi {{customerName}}, we waited for you at {{bookingTime}} for {{serviceName}} at {{businessName}}. Hope everything is okay. We have freed your slot. Reply with a new date and time and we will fix it for you.

Example: A customer has a 4pm hair colour slot and does not arrive by 4:15pm. You send the first WhatsApp asking if she is on the way. At 4:45pm with no reply, you send the reschedule message. The same evening she replies with a new date for Saturday morning. You update the diary and confirm the new slot.
