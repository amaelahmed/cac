## Sample Strategy Blocks

### Use a safe six-step custom order process

**Why we suggest this:** Suggested because your business matches pre launch, customised products, customised product, students, gen z.

**What to do:** Customer sends name, photo, design idea, and delivery date. Shop sends preview. Customer confirms details. Customer pays advance. Product is made. Final photo is sent before delivery.

**Why it matters:** Customers trust the order more when every step is clear.

**Example for this business:** Cartroid can pin this process in Instagram highlights and send it as the first WhatsApp reply.

**How to know if it worked:** Check if customers send complete details faster after reading the process.
### Start with three actions, not a huge plan

**Why we suggest this:** Suggested because your business matches pre launch, customised products, customised product, students, gen z.

**What to do:** Prepare sample products. Write the WhatsApp order steps. Post a simple launch teaser.

**Why it matters:** Simple first actions help you move today instead of waiting for a perfect launch.

**Example for this business:** Cartroid can say: We are opening soon in Kozhikode. Send your idea on WhatsApp, see a preview, and confirm before we make it.

**How to know if it worked:** Check if at least five people ask what you are launching or how to order.
### Send a design preview before making the product

**Why we suggest this:** Suggested because your business matches pre launch, customised products, customised product, students, gen z.

**What to do:** Ask for name, photo, colour, size, and spelling. Send a simple preview image or mockup. Start making only after the customer confirms.

**Why it matters:** This prevents mistakes and makes customers feel safe before paying.

**Example for this business:** Cartroid can reply: I will send a preview first. Please check name, photo, colour, size, and spelling before I make it.

**How to know if it worked:** Check if fewer people ask what happens if the design is wrong.
### Write an Instagram bio that explains ordering

**Why we suggest this:** Suggested because your business matches pre launch, customised products, customised product, students, gen z.

**What to do:** Say what you sell. Mention preview before making. Mention WhatsApp ordering and local delivery.

**Example for this business:** Customised gifts in Kozhikode. Send your idea on WhatsApp. Preview before making. Delivery and pickup available.

**How to know if it worked:** Check if profile visitors start messaging instead of only viewing posts.
### Use a seven-day launch countdown

**Why we suggest this:** Suggested because your business matches pre launch, customised products, customised product, students, gen z.

**What to do:** Post one simple launch update every day for seven days. Show samples, price range, order steps, delivery area, and launch offer. Repeat the same update on WhatsApp status.
